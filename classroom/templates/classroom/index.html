THE RULES

Listen. Recognize. Reveal.

  1. Join the same network

    Open the address on the board. Create a player name and passcode to reuse your profile next time.

  2. Listen to the classroom speakers

    The host plays the music. Enter the anime title on your own device; you can revise it until time runs out.

  3. See the answer together

    Each correct anime earns one point. English, romaji, and known alternative titles are accepted. The host can review an answer.

  4. Make it your playlist

    Add a public MAL username to your profile. The host can build a quiz from everyone's lists, the starter collection, or local files.

Arriving mid-game? You'll spectate until the next quiz. Your passcode belongs to this classroom server.

Reset this room?

The current quiz and scores will be cleared. Saved player profiles will stay.
